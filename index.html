About

Gaza → Dublin

In Gaza, a child will carry a wounded kitten through the rubble because it is the one thing left to protect. We never turn them away.

When the war came, I stayed. For months I was the only vet still working in Gaza, seeing up to a hundred animals a day, from cats carried in by children to the animals of Gaza's zoo. The horses and donkeys came too. With no fuel left, they carry water, food and whole families.

Every animal we heal gives something back to the family around it. Sometimes a child's smile. Sometimes the strength to keep going.

That is why I founded Aman Vet Center in 2022, and why I still lead it from Dublin today.

Far from Gaza. Never away from it.

One Health
